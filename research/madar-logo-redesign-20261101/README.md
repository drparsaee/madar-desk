# Madar Desk logo redesign - concept stage

Original reference: `../../public/madar-icon.svg`, reviewed 2026-11-01.

The installed `logo-design` skill supplied redesign and vector-design guidance.
These are unapproved concept vectors, not replacement application assets.

## Directions

- A: typographic M, simplified blue silhouette with one mint interlock. Closest to a scalable app monogram.
- B: orbital container with integrated M, expressing the name's circular workflow. Most substantial brand change.
- C: flattened geometry of original blue verticals, white flow ribbon and mint finishing ribbon. Highest visual continuity.

Source colours retained: blue `#6094F0`, mint `#67F0CB`, white `#F7FCFF`. Deep navy `#0C1A31` is the intended app icon background.

Next after selection: refine optical balance, produce outlined Persian wordmark and SVG/PNG/ICO variants; validate in the real app UI. The current release assets were not modified.
